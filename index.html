01 / ABOUT ALOK INDUSTRIES

Bridging the gap between education and industry.

ALOK INDUSTRIES is a leading Business Skill Development Centre based in Nagercoil. We specialize in transforming fresh talent into capable business leaders through our intensive training program in Office Administration and Field Administration.

Our mission is simple: to bridge the gap between education and industry. We provide hands-on training in complete business operations — from office management, documentation, filing and professional communication to field execution, vendor coordination and reporting.

We don't just teach theory; we build practical, job-ready skills that companies actually need. Upon successful completion of our program, we provide Assistant Manager Posting, enabling trainees to step directly into leadership and supervisory roles across various industries.

We are not just a training institute; we are a launchpad for your managerial career in Nagercoil, Kanyakumari, and beyond.

02 / ABOUT OUR TRAINING PROGRAM

Professional training focused on real-world management.

Our Professional Training in Business Skills is focused on real-world management. We train you in two practical areas.

▦

Office Administration

You will learn complete office management, documentation & filing, inward/outward handling, attendance & staff coordination, office discipline, report preparation and professional communication.

  • Complete office management
  • Documentation & filing
  • Inward / outward handling
  • Attendance & staff coordination
  • Office discipline
  • Manual report preparation
  • Professional communication
⌁

Field Administration

You will learn complete on-ground execution — site visits, vendor handling, logistics & material movement, daily work reporting, team supervision and client follow-up.

  • Site visits
  • On-ground execution
  • Vendor handling
  • Logistics & material movement
  • Daily work reporting
  • Team supervision
  • Client follow-up
